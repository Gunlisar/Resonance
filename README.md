# Resonance

Resonance — native macOS music player with a personal library, music search, and streaming.

![alt text](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/be8b1d07-5c76-41c6-99ce-81691d623ddc)
- Find artists and albums, browse discographies, and automatically match audio files to track listings.
- Import your own collection, create playlists, manage the play queue, and sync your library across Macs over SMB or NFS.
- Play lossless audio with exclusive bit-perfect output, use DSD over DoP with compatible DACs, and analyze frequency spectra and dynamic range.
- Supported playback formats — FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, AAC, and DSD (DSF and uncompressed DFF).
